# E-Commerce Analytics Project

## Objective

Analyze e-commerce transaction data to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and marketing effectiveness.

## Business Questions

### Sales

1. What is total revenue?
2. What is monthly revenue trend?
3. Which categories generate the most revenue?

### Customers

1. Who are the top customers?
2. Which customer segments are most valuable?

### Products

1. Which products generate the most profit?
2. Which products have the highest sales?

### Marketing

1. Which campaigns perform best?
2. Which traffic sources generate the most revenue?

## KPIs

* Revenue
* Profit
* Profit Margin
* Total Orders
* Average Order Value
* Customer Count
